# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-29)

- Doric, Ionic, Corinthian and Tuscan fronts computed from Vitruvius's rules, with
  every part selectable and named in Greek and Latin.
- The editors' corrections behind Vitruvius's printed totals (27 and 42, 19½ and
  29½, 11½, 18 and 24½), checked against the triglyph and intercolumniation arithmetic.
- Vitruvius's seven temple plans, and two comparisons with the Perseus catalogue:
  flank column counts by date, and column slenderness.
- Schematic plates for Egypt, Mesopotamia, India, China and Tenochtitlan with
  sourced vocabulary.
- A spaced-repetition drill over every deck.
